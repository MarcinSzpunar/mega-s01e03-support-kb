0002 · Match causes before merging reports

owner Marcin Szpunar proposed reviewed 2026-11-02 · 0 days ago

Context

The review of #4471 found separate parser and form-hint causes in one ticket. Subject-only matching concealed that distinction. The executable dedupe explorer already compares tenant-specific causes instead of subjects.

Proposed decision

Replace the operational rule in decision 0001 with evidence-based matching: check tenant locale, booking form, channel and reported behavior before merging. Use subject similarity for discovery, not proof. Deduplicate export rows by report ID independently of merging related reports into a ticket.

Consequences

The desk must read enough evidence to distinguish causes. Unknown cases remain visibly unresolved. Existing accepted decisions are not rewritten; this proposal requires desk acceptance before it replaces their policy.
